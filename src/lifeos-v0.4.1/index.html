Release Notes

AEGIS LifeOS v0.4.1
Added in v0.4.1: iPhone safe-area support, larger touch targets, corrected four-button mobile navigation, visible local-data status, guarded storage failures, stricter encrypted-backup validation, multi-tab vault locking, hosted Content Security Policy, and automated regression checks. The approved v0.4 interface and encrypted-vault format remain unchanged.

Still not connected: social account data, YouTube account history, Gmail, Calendar, financial accounts, health devices, voice wake word, purchases, messaging, and device control. Launching a site does not grant AEGIS access to it.
