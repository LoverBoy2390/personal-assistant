## Locked interpretation boundary

The animation may be dramatically stronger than a future raw wearable signal. The interface must always state that visualization intensity is amplified and does not represent medical severity. In this build every value is synthetic.

## Locked exclusions

- No wearable, smart accessory, health record, biometric, or personal data.
- No medical diagnosis, medical monitoring claim, or emergency determination.
- No AWS SDK, account, resource, credential, deployment, or charge.
- No OAuth, connector, provider token, background synchronization, or external request.
- No purchase, transfer, message, deletion, or account modification.
- No production merge or deployment.
